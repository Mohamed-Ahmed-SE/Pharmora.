# Design system

This implementation follows `pharma_nextjs_codex_kit/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `CODEX_BUILD_PROMPT.md`. Keep the public surface light, editorial, and corporate: background `#F8FBFC`, white surfaces, navy `#09243A`, medical blue `#1769AA`, aqua `#31C7C5`, light aqua `#EAF8F7`, text `#17212B`, muted text `#667581`, and border `#DDE7EA`.

Use generous whitespace, strong readable headings, flat bordered layouts, restrained accents, pharmaceutical/laboratory photography, and mobile-first composition. The admin uses a separate, compact navy-and-neutral workspace with clear tables and restrained controls. Avoid terminal/dark public styling, glow, excessive gradients, glass-heavy treatments, unverified metrics, clinical claims, fabricated company facts, and decorative motion.

Headings prefer Manrope with Avenir Next/Arial fallbacks; body copy prefers Inter with system sans-serif fallbacks. No externally hosted font is loaded by this starter. All content in the demo brand, products, jobs, and newsroom is illustrative and must be replaced with organization-approved material.
