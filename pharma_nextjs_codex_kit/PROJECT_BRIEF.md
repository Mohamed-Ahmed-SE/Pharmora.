# Project Brief

## Product
Premium pharmaceutical corporate website with a custom CMS/admin dashboard.

## Business Goals
1. Establish credibility and trust.
2. Present the company, manufacturing capability, quality systems, and research clearly.
3. Publish a structured pharmaceutical product catalog.
4. Give each product a dedicated SEO-friendly URL.
5. Allow internal staff to manage products, news, careers, certifications, and homepage content without editing code.
6. Make the website fast, responsive, accessible, and easy to maintain.

## Brand Personality
- Scientific
- Precise
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- International
- Professional
- Calm

## Avoid
- excessive gradients
- neon/glow effects
- glassmorphism-heavy interfaces
- fake 3D medical illustrations
- overly rounded SaaS cards
- excessive motion
- generic AI-looking icons
- cluttered layouts
- unverified medical claims

## UX Principles
- strong hierarchy
- clear navigation
- generous spacing
- simple content scanning
- obvious CTA hierarchy
- structured pharmaceutical data
- mobile-first responsive behavior
