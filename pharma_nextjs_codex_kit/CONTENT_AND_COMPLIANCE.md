# Content and Pharmaceutical Compliance Notes

This website must not invent or publish unverified pharmaceutical claims.

## Rules
- Do not generate fake efficacy claims.
- Do not fabricate approvals, registrations, GMP certificates, countries, manufacturing capacity, or clinical claims.
- Demo content must be clearly identifiable as placeholder/demo content.
- Production content must come from the client/company.
- Product leaflets and registration data should be uploaded from verified company sources.
- Regulatory wording may differ by country.

## Product Content Structure
Keep product pages structured rather than free-form:
- Trade Name
- Generic Name
- Active Ingredient
- Strength
- Dosage Form
- Packaging
- Therapeutic Area
- Description
- Leaflet
- Registration Information if applicable

## CMS Principle
Admins should edit structured fields.
Avoid a fully unrestricted page builder for regulated product information.
